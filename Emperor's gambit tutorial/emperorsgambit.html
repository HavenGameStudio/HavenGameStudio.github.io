Emperor's Gambit: The Fog of War

Strategic Hidden-Information Fantasy Board Game

Emperor's Gambit: The Fog of War is a strategic, hidden-information board game designed for mobile platforms. It combines competitive tactics with fantasy warfare, Warlord abilities, unit promotions, and a mana-based skill system. Players must outsmart their opponent by eliminating the enemy Emperor — or escorting their own Emperor deep into enemy territory.

🎯 Objective

  • Defeat the enemy Emperor
  • Move your Emperor to the enemy backline
  • Win by opponent timeout

🗺️ Game Board

  • Mobile-optimized rectangular board
  • 74 total tiles across 10 rows
  • Buff tiles (teleport portals) appear after 20 moves
  • No diagonal movement unless granted by skills

🛡️ Units

Unit Quantity Weakness (Defeated By)
Champion 1 Assassin
Knight 1 Champion, Assassin
Swordsman 1 Knight, Champion, Assassin
Spearman 1 Swordsman, Knight, Champion, Assassin
Axeman 1 Spearman, Swordsman, Knight, Champion, Assassin
Crossbow 1 Axeman and all higher ranks
Archer 2 Crossbow and all higher ranks
Scout 2 Archer and all higher ranks
Footman 3 Scout and all higher ranks
Apprentice 8 All units except Assassin
Assassin 2 Apprentice
Emperor 1 All units (cannot attack)

Total Units: 24 per player

Special Rule: Same rank clash destroys both units

🕹️ Core Mechanics

Combat Reveal

  • Only the losing piece is removed
  • Winner identity remains hidden

Movement

  • Move 1 tile (cardinal directions)
  • Diagonal movement via skills only

Setup Phase

  • 60 seconds to place units
  • Preset layouts supported

🧙 Warlord System

Choose from 10 Warlords each with 2 unique abilities powered by a mana system.

  • Gain 1 mana per turn
  • Skills have cost and usage limits
  • Use skill and move in the same turn

Starter Warlords

🟢 Warlord Green – The Wild Summoner
Nature’s Reinforcement
10 Mana • 1 Use
Summon 1 random piece (Apprentice, Scout, or Archer) to your backline.
Forest Rebirth
12 Mana • 1 Use
Revive a fallen Apprentice, Footman, Scout, or Archer to your base.
⚔️ Warlord Richard – The Blade Commander
Battle Shift
3 Mana • 6 Uses
Move any piece diagonally by 1 tile.
King’s Advance
10 Mana • 1 Use
Move any piece diagonally up to 2 tiles (max to 3rd rank).
🔥 Warlord Ember – The Flame Instigator
Blazing Swap
2 Mana • 5 Uses
Swap two adjacent friendly units.
Sacrificial Blaze
8 Mana • 2 Uses
Destroy one of your units to summon a Footman at the center row.
🧠 Warlord Sage – The Mind Architect
Visionary Recall
4 Mana • 5 Uses
Reveal a random enemy piece for 2 turns.
Strategic Mirror
15 Mana • 1 Use
Copy the opponent’s last used Warlord skill.

⏱️ Timer System

  • 5 minutes per player
  • +2 seconds increment per turn
  • Pauses during animations and skills
  • Timeout = instant loss

🏆 Win Conditions

  • Defeat the enemy Emperor
  • Escort your Emperor to the enemy backline
  • Opponent timer reaches 0
